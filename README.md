Hi, my name is Claudio Yruegas, and I am a recent graduate from the McCombs School of Business with a BBA in Business Analytics and a minor in Wealth Management. I am interested in working at the intersection of Finance and Data Analytics. Some other interests of mine include learning more about how AI is developed, how it works, and how it can be effectively implemented in the workplace to enhance efficiency and productivity.



This repository serves as my journal and documentation of my progress in what I am learning, what I have learned, and the current projects I am pursuing. The journal will be partitioned by 3-4 reports per week of my progress, what I found interesting, and what I found difficult to understand/learn. Feel free to check my profile and my projects, or look at my LinkedIn to see my education and certifications.


This repository also contains a section of project reports that contains the project objective, what I learned from each project, and what I found challenging about it. I will also include links to topics/books that I have been reading to improve my understanding of these topics.
